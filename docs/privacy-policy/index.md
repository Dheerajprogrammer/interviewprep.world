# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 7, 2026_

InterviewPrep.World respects your privacy. This policy explains the limited information that may be handled when you use this website.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your name, email address, and enquiry. We use it only to respond to your request or to improve the site.

The site may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate usage, such as popular pages, device type, and approximate location. This helps us improve content and site performance. Analytics are not used to sell personal information.

Some services connected to the site, including analytics, advertising, sponsorship, or embedded third-party links, may set cookies or process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies before interacting with their services.

## Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Contact emails are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, or meet legal obligations.

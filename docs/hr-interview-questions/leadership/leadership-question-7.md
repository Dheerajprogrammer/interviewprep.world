# How do you make decisions with incomplete information?

## Answer

State the reversible and irreversible parts, gather enough evidence for the cost of delay, consult the right experts, choose a direction, and define signals that would trigger a revisit. Do not wait for certainty when learning is possible.

## Example

```text
I set a clear quality bar, paired with two engineers on the approach,
and used a lightweight review checklist. The team later adopted it for all releases.
```

Explain how you created alignment and enabled others, not just what you personally delivered.


## Practical considerations

1. Choose the approach from the requirement and constraints, not from habit.
2. Include validation, error handling, and cleanup where the boundary requires them.
3. Verify the observable result with focused tests or measurement.

## In practice

For this easy-level topic, make assumptions explicit, choose the smallest safe implementation, and verify the behavior at the relevant boundary.

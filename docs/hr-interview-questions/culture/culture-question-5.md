# What does inclusion mean to you?

## Answer

Inclusion means designing team practices so people with different backgrounds, communication styles, and circumstances can contribute and influence decisions. Give an example such as sharing context asynchronously, rotating facilitation, or crediting ideas fairly.

## Example

```text
I do my best work in a candid, low-ego environment: people challenge ideas
directly, share context early, and follow through on commitments.
```

Make your answer specific and positive; avoid presenting a preference as a criticism of past teams.


## Practical considerations

1. Choose the approach from the requirement and constraints, not from habit.
2. Include validation, error handling, and cleanup where the boundary requires them.
3. Verify the observable result with focused tests or measurement.

## In practice

For this easy-level topic, make assumptions explicit, choose the smallest safe implementation, and verify the behavior at the relevant boundary.

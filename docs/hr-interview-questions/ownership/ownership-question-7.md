# Describe a time you disagreed with a decision.

## Answer

Explain the decision fairly, state your concern with evidence, and show how you raised it through the right channel. Once a decision was made, demonstrate commitment unless new facts created a material risk.

## Example

```text
Situation: A release was at risk because a dependency changed late.
Action: I surfaced the risk, proposed a fallback, and coordinated a scoped release.
Result: We met the date without compromising the critical workflow.
```

Ownership means making the next safe action clear, not silently taking every task yourself.


## Practical considerations

1. Choose the approach from the requirement and constraints, not from habit.
2. Include validation, error handling, and cleanup where the boundary requires them.
3. Verify the observable result with focused tests or measurement.

## In practice

For this hard-level topic, make assumptions explicit, choose the smallest safe implementation, and verify the behavior at the relevant boundary.

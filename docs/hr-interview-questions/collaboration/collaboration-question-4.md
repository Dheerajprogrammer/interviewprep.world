# Tell me about a time you influenced without authority.

## Answer

Describe how you built credibility with data, customer impact, prototypes, or one-on-one conversations rather than relying on title. Explain the stakeholders, objections, compromise, and result.

## Example

```text
Situation: API and UI teams disagreed on the error contract.
Task: Ship without hiding actionable failures.
Action: I proposed examples, facilitated a short decision meeting, and documented the contract.
Result: We shipped on time and reduced support tickets by 30%.
```

Use concrete actions and results; do not frame teammates as the problem.


## Practical considerations

1. Choose the approach from the requirement and constraints, not from habit.
2. Include validation, error handling, and cleanup where the boundary requires them.
3. Verify the observable result with focused tests or measurement.

## In practice

For this medium-level topic, make assumptions explicit, choose the smallest safe implementation, and verify the behavior at the relevant boundary.

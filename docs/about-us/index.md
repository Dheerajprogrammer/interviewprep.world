# About InterviewPrep.World

InterviewPrep.World helps developers prepare for technical and behavioural interviews with clear questions, practical answers, and small examples that make each concept easier to discuss.

Our learning paths cover React, Angular, JavaScript, TypeScript, frontend system design, and HR interview preparation. The content is organised so you can review a topic quickly, understand the reasoning behind an answer, and practise explaining it in your own words.

## Our approach

- Focus on concepts commonly discussed in real interviews.
- Explain the answer before showing a concise example.
- Keep related questions together so you can study a subject progressively.
- Treat the site as a learning resource, not a substitute for hands-on practice and project experience.

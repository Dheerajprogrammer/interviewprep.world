# Resume Preparation

Structure impact-driven bullets, quantify outcomes, and align keywords with the roles you target.

## Checklist

- One page for most candidates under 10 years of experience
- Link GitHub/LinkedIn; omit unrelated hobbies
- Lead with outcomes: latency reduced, revenue influenced, users served
